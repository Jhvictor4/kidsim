# KidSim demo video — design

## Style Prompt
Warm, paper-like cards lifted straight from the in-game UI: cream card on a deep warm-ink stage, one orange accent, confident geometric sans headlines. Calm, kid-friendly but not childish; motion is quick and soft (power3/expo outs), never bouncy.

## Colors
- Stage background: #1E1A16 (warm ink)
- Card / light surface: #F6F1E7 (game card cream)
- Ink text on cream: #211C17
- Accent: #D9772B (game orange — eyebrows, highlights, one key word)
- Muted text on cream: #6E665C
- Success green (growth numbers only): #2F7D4E

## Typography
- Headlines + UI: "Montserrat", 800 (matches the game UI)
- Supporting lines: "Fraunces", italic 400

## What NOT to Do
- No gradients on text, no neon, no cyan/purple.
- No pure #000 / #fff.
- No bouncy/elastic easing.
- No invented features: packs other than Cafe Shift are labeled "next".
- No forced <br> line breaks.
